Privacy and local processing

Tool input is processed locally in your browser and is not uploaded to UtilCover servers.

Local processing

Text and files supplied to a tool are processed by JavaScript running on your device. UtilCover has no tool-processing API. Closing or reloading a page clears in-memory tool data; favorites and recent tools, when enabled, remain only in your browser’s localStorage until you clear them.

Network requests

Your browser requests the site’s HTML, CSS, JavaScript, icons and locally hosted QR library. UtilCover does not currently use analytics, tracking pixels, advertising, third-party fonts, or third-party CDNs. Tool input is not included in these requests.

Limits

Your browser, extensions, network provider, or a modified copy of the site may behave differently. Use the official HTTPS site and inspect its network activity if your threat model requires it.
