    Split PDF pages privately in your browser

    Enter comma-separated page numbers or continuous ranges. Each entry becomes its own PDF in the order entered. For example, 1-3, 5, 8-10 creates files containing pages 1–3, page 5, and pages 8–10. The PDF library and worker are served by this site; your document bytes are processed locally and are not sent to a server, third-party API, or CDN.

    How to split a PDF

    1. Select one PDF.The file is checked locally. Password-protected PDFs and documents outside the published size and page limits are not supported.
    2. Enter the page ranges.Separate each page number or continuous range with a comma. A range such as 2-5 stays together in one output; 2-5, 8 creates two files. Pages cannot appear in more than one range.
    3. Split and download.Select Split PDF, then download each generated file. Output filenames are based on the selected page ranges, not on your source filename.

    Page-range examples

    Separate chapters

    Enter 1-12, 13-24 to create two PDFs, one for each chapter range. Output order follows the range list.

    Extract selected pages

    Enter 2, 7, 10-12 to create one-page files for pages 2 and 7 plus one file containing pages 10–12.

    Supported files and limits

    This tool accepts one readable, unencrypted PDF up to 25 MiB and 500 pages. You can enter up to 20 comma-separated ranges; the total number of pages across all outputs is limited to 500, all outputs together are limited to 60 MiB, and processing stops after 45 seconds. Invalid, overlapping, or out-of-range page selections are rejected locally.

    Each output is a newly written PDF containing the selected pages. PDF page extraction does not guarantee preservation of interactive forms, bookmarks, attachments, document metadata, or digital signatures. Existing signatures cannot remain valid after rewriting. Review the results and keep the original document.

    Files are temporarily read into browser memory and passed to a same-origin worker. They are not uploaded, stored in browser storage, or saved to recent-tool history. Selections and generated download links are cleared when leaving the page or returning through browser history.

    PDF splitter FAQ

    Are my PDFs uploaded?

    No. File reading and page extraction run locally in your browser and its same-origin worker. PDF bytes are not sent to a server, third-party API, or CDN and are not saved in browser storage or recent-tool history.

    How does the comma-separated range field work?

    Each comma-separated item creates one output file. For example, 1-3, 5, 8-10 creates three PDFs. Ranges are kept in the order entered and cannot overlap.

    Can I extract a single page?

    Yes. Enter a page number such as 7. The output is a one-page PDF named page-7.pdf.

    Can I split a password-protected PDF?

    No. Password-protected PDFs are not supported. Do not enter or share a document password with this tool.
